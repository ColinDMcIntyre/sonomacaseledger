Editorial accountability

Corrections & source updates

Court outcomes can change after sentencing or news coverage. The archive welcomes verifiable corrections and later court orders.

How to report an issue

Open a public GitHub correction request. Include the affected case URL, the exact statement needing correction, the date of the relevant court action, and a public court or government source when available.

You can search the Sonoma Superior Court case portal for up-to-date public case information. This website does not operate the court's records service.

Privacy and source handling

Do not post sealed, confidential, private or unredacted personal records to a public GitHub issue. Court documents will be screened for publication suitability. An individual's account of a case is not a substitute for a court order.

Our correction process

  1. Identify the exact published statement and supporting source.
  2. Verify the jurisdiction, case number and relevant court dates, including later appeals, reductions and dismissals.
  3. Update the source register and the public summary with a current review date.
  4. Preserve a transparent revision history in GitHub.

Source grades classify sources, not guilt or the completeness of docket review. Source register last documented: November 18, 2026.
