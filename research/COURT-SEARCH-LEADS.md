## Rules for promoting a lead into a verified case number

1. Open the named case ID in the **official** [Sonoma Superior Court case portal](https://sonoma.courts.ca.gov/online-services/case-portal), not a generic third-party docket site.
2. Confirm full defendant name, **criminal** case type, alleged charge(s), and the incident's approximate date, business or victim (only as publicly shown).
3. Check dated plea/verdict, original sentence, amended disposition, and any Penal Code §1203.4 relief; do not confuse a post-conviction dismissal with an acquittal.
4. Keep the court-portal PDF in the original research folder, and do not publish a raw copy without privacy screening.
5. Only then populate `caseNo` in the public case data and update the profile.
