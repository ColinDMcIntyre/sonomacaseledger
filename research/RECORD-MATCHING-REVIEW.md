## E. What is finished, and what is not

**Finished in this review:** source-file classification for all 28 PDFs; exclusion of the eight different proceedings; identification of the six search-result lists; case-number matching for 14 court-detail exports; checking key later events in the nine longer matching documents, with the two additional §1203.4 dismissals confirmed.

**Not finished:** line-by-line, full-page privacy clearance of the nine remaining matching court-detail exports (430 pages). Most of these documents have no extractable text and may include details concerning victims, witnesses or private addresses. Page samples and date/event checks are not sufficient to certify an unredacted public release. **Do not publish these originals or imply complete privacy screening.**

**Case coverage:** 18 of 31 county profiles still lack a matched criminal docket from the supplied collection. That is an unresolved verification status, not evidence the case did not happen.

**Prepared:** November 19, 2026. This is an informational source audit; case-status portal exports are not certified judgments.
