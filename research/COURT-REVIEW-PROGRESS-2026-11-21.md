## Publication gate still in force

Do **not** upload the nine unredacted originals. Before publishing a reproduction:

1. Examine every original page at legible resolution for protected/private identifiers, victim and witness details, contact data, and nonpublic records.
2. Verify count-specific findings, sentencing, appeals and later relief rather than relying on an initial portal status label.
3. Redact the *underlying content* (not a cosmetic overlay), remove original PDF metadata, and check the rendered public copy page by page.
4. Keep the source original private, label reproductions as informational, and distinguish complete redacted copies from single-page excerpts.
