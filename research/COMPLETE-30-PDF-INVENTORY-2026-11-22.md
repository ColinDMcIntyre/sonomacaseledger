## Privacy-release standards

**Only the original files containing the six already-published full reproductions had prior page-by-page public-copy review.** Their published versions are transformed and redacted; they are not the original unredacted sources. The nine other matching original PDFs must not be described as privacy-cleared. They span **430 pages** and include **339 image-only pages** plus **91 text-bearing pages**. Mere presence/absence of extractable text is not a privacy determination.

For any new *full* reproduction, inspect each source page at legible resolution; examine victims, witnesses, contact/address information, confidential/sealed items, juvenile/private identifiers; remove content, not cosmetic overlays; rasterize as needed; verify the resulting page count and every rendered output page; ensure no original metadata/searchable hidden layer remains. If this cannot be completed, publish only a clearly labeled, independently screened **selected-page excerpt** or editorial summary, never a document described as a full redacted docket.

**The name-search and unrelated PDFs are NOT candidates for publication** as evidence supporting the existing case profiles even if their filenames or parties resemble those subjects. Separate offenses and proceedings cannot be substituted for missing records.
