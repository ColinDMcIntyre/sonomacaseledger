# Sonoma Case Ledger research register

Date: November 18, 2026.

The archive comprises 30 new Sonoma-area financial-crime profiles plus one existing court-record review of Constantine William Petros, and six separate federal profiles. This is not a countywide largest-case ranking and its financial figures cannot all be compared.

Evidence A = primarily a government, court or appellate source. A/B = government or court material plus published media reporting. B = published reporting with full docket unverified. A; docket pending = state regulator annual report reviewed but a matching Sonoma criminal docket has not been independently checked. A; later relief unverified = a report mentions conditional potential relief, which must not be represented as granted.

The developer also worked on Constantine Petros's personal website. This publication is not affiliated with any court or government agency.

See evidence-table.csv and case-data.json for names, links, and case-specific verification notes. Do not publish new acquittal, exoneration, time-served or other claims without documentary support.
