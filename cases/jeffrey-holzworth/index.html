What happened in this case?

Jeffrey Holzworth received a four-year prison sentence in 2014 following plea-based convictions for grand theft and receiving stolen property. The docket records a denied resentencing petition and later appellate affirmances.

What was alleged
The charges concerned grand theft and receiving stolen property tied to campus parking revenue.
Plea or verdict
The Sonoma docket records plea-based convictions for grand theft and 11 receiving-stolen-property counts. Existing source summaries differ on the plea wording; the precise plea form is not asserted here.
Sentence or restitution
A four-year prison sentence was imposed in 2014. Court minutes record $285,453.49 restitution to Zurich Recovery Services.
Later developments
A resentencing petition was denied in 2015, followed by appellate affirmance entries in 2016–2017. This is separate from Karen Holzworth's record.
