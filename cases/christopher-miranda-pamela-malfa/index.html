Christopher John Miranda and Pamela Malfa

Classic-car restoration theft

What happened in this case?

Reporting describes separate no-contest pleas by Christopher Miranda and Pamela Malfa. Miranda received 15 years four months; Malfa received jail and probation. Matching criminal dockets remain unverified.

What was alleged
Prosecutors described a classic-car restoration scheme involving customer property and funds.
Plea or verdict
Reporting says Miranda pleaded no contest; Malfa separately entered no-contest pleas to grand theft. These are different defendant outcomes.
Sentence or restitution
Miranda reportedly received 15 years four months and was ordered to pay about $850,000 restitution. Malfa reportedly received jail and probation; do not attribute Miranda's amount or sentence to her.
Later developments
Malfa's candidate felony docket number remains unverified at the charge/disposition level; any later relief is unknown.
