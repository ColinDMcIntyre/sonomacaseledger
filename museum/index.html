VISITOR GUIDE

How to Explore

WASD or arrow keys move relative to the camera. E opens the nearest exhibit when you are close enough.

Click or tap the floor to walk automatically. Click an exhibit to approach and open it; manual movement cancels an automatic route.

Drag an empty area to rotate the third-person camera horizontally and vertically. Scroll or use + / − to change follow distance; Follow/Wide changes the viewing distance.

Cases & search opens the collapsible case directory, or press F to find a case. Use Museum Wings to change galleries.

Use the Text Archive for direct, accessible access to every case page.

This museum is an exploratory interface, not a court website. Exhibits reference documented legal records and linked reporting.
