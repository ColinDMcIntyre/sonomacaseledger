# Unified cathedral bay construction

The visual shell now shares one mathematical barrel-vault profile per room, defined in `museum/vault-profile.js`. The 60 m grand nave has eight equally spaced 7.5 m structural bays. Side galleries, the federal apse and vestibule reuse the same architecture language at their original room scales.

**Connected structure:** Each plaster ceiling reaches the interior face of its stone side walls. Every north/south exposed end wall receives a real stone gable cut to the exact ceiling curve. Stone ribs follow the same curve; matching floor-to-spring-line columns have capitals that physically intersect the ribs. The short corridor into the Records Library receives stone side walls and an enclosed plaster roof.

**Uniform finish:** Warm sandstone PBR for walls and gables, paler cut-stone pilasters, capitals, arches and cornices, dark walnut lower wall panels on both sides of the masonry, and a plaster ceiling throughout. No elevated walnut beams pretending to support the stone vault.

**Preserved systems:** Unobstructed arched doorways and portal collision, approved nonoverlapping floor planes and material, stained glass, all 37 case exhibits, furniture import and controls. Navigation collision for the pillars now uses the exact coordinates of their rendered stone shafts.

**Validation:** Automated tests check arch-to-wall spring lines, identical end-gable/ceiling coordinates, column/rib connections, clear portal crossings, and retained floor rules. Browser visuals and performance still need a live playtest.
