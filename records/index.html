Why most original PDFs remain withheld

On November 19, 2026, the researcher supplied 28 court-portal PDFs. They include matched criminal dockets, search-result lists, and unrelated civil, family or older criminal matters. A portal printout is not a certified judgment. Some scanned PDFs may contain sensitive information, and the larger exports have not been cleared page by page for public publication.

Five cases now have public, privacy-redacted, fully rasterized reproductions of their court-portal printouts. All 28 unredacted source originals remain withheld, and the larger unreviewed files have not been made public. This register identifies the court numbers that have been matched to relevant case summaries. It does not imply every hearing, charge or later filing has been reviewed. For certified or current copies, use the official Sonoma Superior Court case portal or contact the clerk. Submit corrections.

Reviewed public copies

5 full-length public reproductions (44 original pages): Petros, Cabudoy, Rodgers, Fatu and Farias. Every copy includes a source-provenance page. The original court printouts are withheld. No judgment is certified by this archive.

Files that do not verify the intended criminal case

Eight supplied PDFs concern different court proceedings, and six more are search lists rather than case histories. Those records are intentionally excluded as evidence for the reported incidents. A match pending here does not mean the reported case never happened.

What counts as a match

A matching case-detail export should identify the defendant, Sonoma Superior Court criminal jurisdiction and the charge or incident described in the original report. A case number belonging to someone with the same name is not enough.

For example, the early search found a family-law case for Michael Rosen, civil records for several other names, and unrelated criminal cases for some insurance-regulator entries. Those results are deliberately not presented as criminal-docket verification.

Original research and document-intake notes are available in the public source-audit report. The 28-PDF intake manifest also documents which scanned files were matched, excluded or held for review.
