Research standards and accountability

Editorial methodology

An independent, non-exhaustive archive of selected cases. Not a court website or a live criminal-record search.

Scope and selection

The archive collects selected Sonoma County and Sonoma-connected federal cases. Inclusion is not a ranking, an indication of current legal status, or a claim that a person committed every alleged offense.

Evidence and verification

  1. Identify the relevant court, jurisdiction, case identifier where known, and source date. Do not treat similar names or regulator identifiers as confirmed criminal docket matches.
  2. Distinguish court dockets and orders from government announcements and published reporting. Source grades describe the kind of evidence, not guilt or the completeness of review.
  3. Describe allegations, pleas or verdicts, sentencing, and later relief separately. Attribute assertions to their sources and state what has not been verified.
  4. Link the supporting public materials. Where a court printout is published, identify its scope and privacy redactions. Consult the official court for certified or updated records.

Review the court-record matching and verification register and source-grade definitions.

Legal terminology

Allegation or charge
An assertion or formal accusation, not by itself a finding of guilt.
Plea or verdict
A plea is a defendant's formal response to a charge; a verdict is a determination following trial. The case profile describes the documented disposition.
Conviction
A recorded finding of guilt or judgment following a qualifying plea or verdict. Later relief may change its legal consequences.
Dismissal
A court action ending a charge or proceeding; its meaning depends on the timing, legal basis and actual order.
Post-conviction relief
A later court action affecting an existing conviction or sentence. California Penal Code section 1203.4 dismissal is not itself an acquittal or a finding of factual innocence.
Restitution and financial amounts
Restitution ordered by a court is distinct from reported losses, allegations, requested amounts and proposed plea terms.

Independence, funding and conflicts

Sonoma Case Ledger is privately maintained and is not affiliated with Sonoma County Superior Court or any government agency. The developer has a professional relationship with Constantine Petros, including work on his personal website. Readers should independently verify case outcomes against the linked primary records. Detailed ownership and funding information is not specified here.

Corrections, updates and privacy

Source dates are not assurances that a record remains current. Editorial changes are distinct from new court-record reviews. The archive provides a corrections process for documented errors and later orders. Do not submit sealed or private records in public issues.

Official sources

Use the Sonoma County Superior Court public case portal for current public case information and contact the appropriate court for certified records. This archive is informational, not legal advice.
