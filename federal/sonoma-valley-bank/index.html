Sonoma Valley Bank — Sean Cutting, Brian Melland and David Lonich

Federal bank fraud

Evidence AUS federal court
Case outcome

Documented disposition

After a federal trial, Cutting and Melland each received 100 months, and Lonich received 80 months in prison.

Case period2018
Reported amountAt least $47 million
Evidence levelA

Case background

DOJ attributed about $39.18 million in losses to the FDIC and $8.65 million to TARP. This case was prosecuted in federal court, not Sonoma Superior Court.

Understanding the financial figure

At least $47 million: Losses identified by federal prosecutors. Amounts on this site may be losses, allegations, restitution, attempted transactions or plea-agreement figures and should not be treated as directly comparable.
