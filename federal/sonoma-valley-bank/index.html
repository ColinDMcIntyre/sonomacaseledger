Sonoma Valley Bank — Sean Cutting, Brian Melland and David Lonich

Federal bank fraud

Source grade AUS federal court
At a glance · allegation, outcome, and later actions

What happened in this case?

Federal bank-fraud convictions involving three defendants resulted in prison sentences.

What was alleged
Federal prosecutors described a bank-related fraud scheme that caused substantial public-insurance and financial-assistance losses.
Plea or verdict
Following a federal trial, Sean Cutting, Brian Melland and David Lonich were convicted, according to the cited federal prosecution account.
Sentence or restitution
Cutting and Melland each received 100-month sentences; Lonich received 80 months. Federal prosecutors attributed approximately $39.18 million in FDIC losses and $8.65 million in TARP losses.
Later developments
Later appeals or other relief have not been independently verified in this archive. This was not a Sonoma Superior Court prosecution.
