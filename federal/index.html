What the source grades mean

A: A government, court, regulatory or appellate source supports the cited facts. A/B: That material is supplemented by reporting. B: Published reporting is the main source and the underlying trial-court docket has not been independently checked.

Important: A grade is not a finding of guilt, a guarantee that all claims were independently confirmed, or proof that the complete docket was reviewed. Qualifications such as “docket pending” and “later relief unverified” matter.
