Research standards

How cases are documented

Case entries distinguish allegations, pleas, adjudicated outcomes, and later orders. A case marked “dismissed” may have a very different procedural history from one resolved by an acquittal.

Source grades: A means cited government, court or regulatory material; A/B combines that material with published reporting; B relies mainly on reporting. A grade does not mean the full court docket has been reviewed. Each case page states its docket status separately.

Last documented source review: November 18, 2026. This archive is not a live court-status service. Report a correction or use the Sonoma court-record lookup checklist.

  • Use the applicable court's public case index or records as the primary source for reported docket events.
  • Give exact dates and legal grounds for dismissals and post-conviction relief.
  • Label reported financial figures according to what they represent, such as alleged claim value, proven loss, or restitution.
  • Avoid conclusions about innocence, guilt, or time served beyond what the cited record establishes.

About this project

Publicly accessible research, privately maintained.

Sonoma Case Ledger is a privately produced informational archive, not a service of the Sonoma County Superior Court or any government agency. The developer of this site has also worked on Constantine Petros's personal website; the case write-up here is based on the cited public court docket rather than an independent investigation.

The public portal is an informational service and is not a substitute for certified court records. For current or official records, contact the court clerk.
